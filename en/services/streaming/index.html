What is Streaming Acceleration?

Streaming acceleration is a CDN service purpose-built for live and on-demand video. It distributes streams from edge nodes close to each viewer, absorbs sudden traffic spikes with elastic bandwidth, and keeps playback smooth by routing around congestion, while hotlink protection and stream-level controls guard the content itself.

ByteShield runs streaming acceleration on a global edge network with Tbps-level elastic bandwidth, so large concurrent audiences and burst traffic do not cause buffering. Intelligent bandwidth allocation keeps HD playback stable across devices and network conditions, and multi-layer hotlink protection with instant stream blocking protects live and on-demand content from piracy and unauthorized rebroadcasting.
