What is a Secure CDN (SCDN)?

A secure CDN (SCDN) is a content delivery network with security built into every edge node: the same infrastructure that caches and accelerates your website also filters DDoS floods, CC attacks, and hotlinking before they reach your origin. Instead of stacking a separate CDN and a separate security vendor, one platform handles routing, caching, and protection together, so acceleration never comes at the cost of safety.

ByteShield delivers SCDN through more than 1,000 secure edge nodes across 250+ cities on six continents, backed by 50 Tbps+ of network bandwidth. Enterprise-grade authoritative DNS with intelligent routing sends each visitor to the fastest healthy node, static and dynamic content are optimized separately, and dedicated China services keep mainland users fast and protected.
