What is DDoS Protection?

DDoS protection is a cloud security service that absorbs and filters distributed denial-of-service traffic before it reaches your servers, keeping websites, applications, and APIs online during an attack. Effective protection covers the network, transport, and application layers, pairing large-scale traffic scrubbing with a web application firewall (WAF) that blocks request-level exploits such as SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, and CC attacks.

ByteShield provides this multi-layer defense through its global edge network and has successfully mitigated DDoS attacks as large as 43 Tbps, with unlimited mitigation capacity included in every plan. Smart detection intercepts new and variant attacks, 0-day vulnerabilities are patched in seconds, and an SDK extends the same protection into mobile apps, keeping your business online 24/7.
