What is a Media Streaming CDN?

A media streaming CDN distributes video and rich media from edge nodes close to each viewer, so high-concurrency audiences stream in HD without overloading any single node or the origin. It absorbs prime-time and viral traffic spikes with elastic bandwidth and protects content from hotlinking and unauthorized rebroadcasting.

ByteShield delivers media over global edge nodes with T-level elastic bandwidth and premium cross-border links, keeping playback low-latency for audiences worldwide. A highly scalable architecture stays stable under sudden load, multi-layer hotlink protection guards copyright, and cross-border acceleration brings smooth viewing to users in hard-to-reach regions.
