What is an iGaming CDN?

An iGaming CDN is a content delivery and security network tuned for online gaming platforms, where milliseconds of latency and minutes of downtime translate directly into lost players and revenue. It combines low-latency routing to players, DDoS and CC protection sized for the attacks the industry attracts, and stable access in regions where connectivity is unreliable.

ByteShield serves iGaming operators with dedicated IPs that minimize the attack surface and BGP-routed scrubbing clusters that clean DDoS traffic at full strength; the network has mitigated attacks up to 43 Tbps. Elastic bandwidth keeps gameplay stable through traffic peaks, and a 24/7 technical team responds instantly, protecting your gaming brand and revenue.
